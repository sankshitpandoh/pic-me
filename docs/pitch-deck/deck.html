Playmate10
Trust & safety

Safe by design, so we can grow on the Play Store and UPI

🚫 Never explicit

Flirty and warm, but every persona deflects sexual content in character. Required by Play Store, payment partners and IT Act s.67A.

🔞 Adults only

Date-of-birth gate on sign-up. All personas are 18+, and they drop flirting if a user says he is underage.

🤖 Honest AI

Clearly labelled “AI character”. If asked sincerely, she says she is an AI.

💸 No manipulation

Personas never ask for money, UPI, gifts, meetings or personal details, and never pressure users to recharge.

💚 Care built in

Self-harm mentions trigger a caring response and the Tele-MANAS helpline (14416).

🔐 Privacy

OTP login, no public profiles, delete-all-chats and full account deletion in the app.

Playmate11
How it's built

Light app, smart backend

  • React Native (Expo), dark UI tuned for budget Android phones and slow data.
  • Claude (Anthropic) powers the personas, with prompt caching to cut cost per message.
  • Personas as text files: new characters ship without an app update.
  • Credit ledger with automatic refunds when a reply fails.
  • Razorpay UPI with signature-verified payments and webhooks.
Unit economics

₹0.71–0.83 revenue per paid message

Main cost lever is the AI model per message. Levers already in the product:

  • Model is one config switch (premium vs. low-cost tiers).
  • Prompt caching and a capped chat history.
  • Pre-made photos: no image-generation cost.

Cost per message and conversion rates to be measured in the pilot before final pricing.
